Data Analyst Assessment — Section B

Preamble — read before answering

Questions 1–3 below refer to this passage. Northwind Logistics operates warehouses in three regions: Coastal, Valley and Highland.

In 2023 the Coastal region shipped 40,000 orders, Valley shipped 25,000 and Highland 10,000.

In 2024 Coastal shipped 44,000 orders, Valley 35,000 and Highland 12,000.

Valley opened a second sorting hub in March 2024, cutting delivery time from 4 days to 2. Highland relies on a single hub; seasonal road closures delay its winter orders.

Table 1 — Returns

Coastal returns: 1,320 (2024). Valley returns: 700 (2024). Highland returns: 480 (2024).

The return rate is returns divided by orders shipped in the same year.

Question 1

Based on the passage and Table 1, which region had the highest return rate in 2024?

A) Coastal
B) Valley
C) Highland
D) Coastal and Valley are tied

Question 2

True or false: Valley's 2024 delivery time was half of its 2023 delivery time.

A) True
B) False

Question 3

By how many orders did Highland's shipments increase from 2023 to 2024?

A) 1,000
B) 2,000
